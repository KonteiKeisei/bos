# November 14 Group Photograph

![Session 06 - Bull and Finch Group Photo.jpg|Bull and Finch group photograph, November 14, 1986, 9:37 p.m.|650](/img/user/Images/Sessions/Session%2006%20-%20Bull%20and%20Finch%20Group%20Photo.jpg)

Taken at [[Locations/Bull and Finch Pub\|the Bull & Finch]] during [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]]. The photograph shows Chandler, Pierre, Bo, Leonard, and Jacques. Its caption reads November 14, 1986, 9:37 p.m.

The group arranges it after discovering Leonard and Jacques cannot remember Lee. Chandler also writes everyone's names and descriptions in Vietnamese. They move from Warren Tavern before taking the photograph because Chandler doesn't want a record placing them near Ruth's house after his break-in.

The photograph records the five people present. Lee is absent, and it does not restore anyone's missing memories. Chandler buys five shots and a sixth for the photographer, paying $3 by the table's ruling. The photographer is unnamed.
