# FADAR

An improvised warning necklace assembled during [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]] from [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]'s Luminous Hollow specimen and [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]'s necklace chain. Chandler wears it under his shirt.

## Observed Behavior

Within 10 feet of the [[Campaign Notes/Winter Scribing Shard\|Winter Scribing Shard]], the specimen pulses with light, vibrates, and becomes slightly warm. Chandler can feel the buzz against his chest. Moving the shard closer reproduces the effect.

The party hopes it detects relevant Fey activity. Only the shard interaction is confirmed. Carrying that known trigger beside the necklace would keep it active, and final separate storage was not settled in the transcript.

Discreet signal: "Does anything here remind you of home?"

## Session 06

The group remembers the necklace and its code phrase in [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]], but no new activation or broader detection ability is established. Chandler's new magical evidence comes from Detect Magic. Do not treat the lack of a narrated FADAR response as proof about a person or location.
