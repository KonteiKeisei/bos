# Winter Scribing Shard

Painfully cold, pencil-like fragment recovered by [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]] at the recent Winter Wolf crime scene. In [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]], he passes it to [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]], who handles it through fabric and puts it in a specimen container.

Bo's examination finds no ordinary wood or graphite, only a substance resembling immutable ice. It triggers [[Campaign Notes/FADAR\|FADAR]] within 10 feet.

[[NPCs/Harvard/Dr. Richard Sparks\|Dr. Richard Sparks]] identifies it as a writing tool belonging to the Winter figure in Kelsey Carey's drawing, whom he calls Queen Mab's foremost Scout. It can leave the frost-script seen at the monument. No new inscription is made with it this session.
