# The Plough & Stars

**912 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge.** Bar owned by [[NPCs/The Plough & Stars/Seamus Connolly\|Seamus Connolly]], visited on November 14 in [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]]. The sign dates its opening to 1969. Bo eats a $6.95 ribeye and steak fries while Chandler interviews the owner.

## The October 29 Incident

Four men, including regular [[NPCs/Cambridge/Dennis Fahey\|Dennis Fahey]], were found frozen in their seats, along with their drinks and the taps. The building's heat was working. The men survived and were discharged from Cambridge City Hospital.

Chandler's Detect Magic on November 14 reveals Divination and Enchantment at four places along the bar, three patches of Evocation on the mirror, and weaker Evocation on the taps. The writing has been wiped off. Chandler cannot read the vanished symbols.

Seamus's account of finding the men after a late-night smoke break conflicts with the report's morning discovery. He admits his memory of the entire night has worsened since he wiped the mirror's frost markings away. Neither account is established as a deliberate lie. Fahey hasn't been back, and the party still needs to find him.

## People

Seamus Connolly owns and tends the bar. Dennis Fahey is a longstanding regular who worked here when younger. Seamus knows him well enough to let him watch the place during a smoke break.

Seamus refuses Bo's Flintstones vitamins and reveals the revolver under his bar. He has heard the story about a bearded man giving vitamins to children. How that story reached him is not established.
