# Boston Garden

The Bruins' home arena and the party's next planned investigation as of [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]]. [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] suspects it because of its position among mapped cold incidents and the rink's ice. A Winter base here remains a theory.

[[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Harry Sinden\|Harry Sinden]] arranges friends' tickets for the November 15 home game. Jacques must speak with [[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Frank Galloway\|Frank Galloway]] about after-hours rink access. [[NPCs/Harvard/Dr. Richard Sparks\|Dr. Richard Sparks]] encourages pursuing the party's lead; he does not independently confirm what they will find. The rink investigation has not happened yet.

## Session 06: Access Arranged

In [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]], Jacques persuades [[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Frank Galloway\|Frank Gallo]] to let him and four ticketed friends stay after the November 15 game. As the crowd leaves, they must report to the security desk. Frank will take them to a back room, where they wait an hour and a half before meeting him by the rink shield. He will then let them use the ice.

The stated game time is 7:35-10:15 p.m., with arrival around seven. The session ends before the game; the arena has not yet been investigated and a Winter presence is still a theory.
