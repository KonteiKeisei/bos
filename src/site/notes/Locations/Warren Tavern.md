# Warren Tavern

**2 Pleasant Street, Charlestown.** Historic tavern with a green-and-gold sign dating it to 1780 and a notice celebrating its 1986 Best Chowder award. Inside are low wooden beams, cigarette smoke, paper menus, ashtrays, and a crowded bar. First visited in [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]].

## November 14

Jacques, Leonard, Pierre, and Bo get a booth while Chandler goes to collect Ruth Hanlon. The [[NPCs/Warren Tavern/Warren Tavern Waitress\|waitress]] takes their orders. Leonard gets a burger without pickles, fries, and Coke; Pierre gets chowder, haddock, mashed turnip, and Budweiser; Jacques gets chowder, Narragansett, and shepherd's pie. Potato skins with Swiss are shared. Bo orders three whiskey sours; four are served and consumed in play.

Bo becomes a spider in the alley, assisted by Jacques, to watch Chandler's date. Chandler returns alone before they can use the plan. After he reports blood and magical footprints at Ruth's house, the conversation reveals that Leonard and Jacques no longer remember Lee. They decide to take their group photograph elsewhere and leave for the Bull & Finch.
