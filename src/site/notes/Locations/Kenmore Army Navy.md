# Kenmore Army Navy

Surplus shop operated by [[NPCs/Kenmore Army Navy/Ned Kenmore\|Ned Kenmore]]. Visited by [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]], [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]], and [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] on Thursday, November 13 in [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]].

Sells knives, field equipment, and protective gear. Ned brings out Northern Fleet Service Bayonets after Chandler requests iron weapons. Their effectiveness against Fey is not established. Jacques purchases Kevlar for himself and Leonard. The store cannot supply Jacques's oversized longbow arrows and directs ammunition buyers to a gun store.
