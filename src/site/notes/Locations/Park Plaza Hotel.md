# Park Plaza Hotel

[[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques le Coq]] stays in the penthouse suite, with multiple floors, side rooms, a grand piano, hot tub, small pool, and stocked minibar. The party uses it as a meeting place and refuge during [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]].

They assemble and test [[Campaign Notes/FADAR\|FADAR]] here. [[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Harry Sinden\|Harry Sinden]] calls Jacques here about his replacement pager and game tickets. Jacques gives Leonard a Kevlar vest, and [[NPCs/Boston Harbor/Trudy\|Trudy]] enters the suite to defend Pierre when Jacques dunks him in cold water. Hotel fax service is offered for ten cents, but Chandler ultimately delivers his article in person.
