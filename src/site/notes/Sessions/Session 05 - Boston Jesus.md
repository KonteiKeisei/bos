# Session 05 - Boston Jesus

> [!info] Session Details
> **Date Played:** August 30, 2026
> **In-Game Dates:** November 12-14, 1986
> **Locations:** Bo's apartment, O'Malley's precinct, [[Locations/Park Plaza Hotel\|Park Plaza Hotel]], [[Locations/Kenmore Army Navy\|Kenmore Army Navy]], a gun store, Harvard, and the old Custom House site near [[Locations/Old State House\|Old State House]].
> **Players:** [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler T. Harrow]]; [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]]; [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]; [[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]]; [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]. Young is absent. [[Player Characters/Lee Smith\|Lee]] leaves at the start.
> **Previous:** [[Sessions/Session 04 - Roger G-Man\|Session 04 - Roger G-Man]]
> **Next:** [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]]

Bo comes home to a Summer Court hearing in his kitchen. A Harvard professor gets him out of it, then spends Friday frustrating the shit out of him. In between, the party makes FADAR, buys supplies, and fails to find arrows for Jacques's ridiculous bow. Pierre gets drunk in the hotel suite while Leonard nurses the drink he was too polite to refuse. The hockey game will have to wait until next session.

---

## Part One: Bo Is Being Sued

**Wednesday, November 12. 10:41 p.m.** Bo opens his door to find a records office on his kitchen counter. Clerks at miniature typewriters record his arrival. Two of them have bagged the radio from the kombucha puddle as evidence.

[[NPCs/Summer Court/Lady Celadine Ashe\|Lady Celadine Ashe]], a red-haired woman in a burgundy suit, represents the Summer Court. Bo is accused of throwing out a credentialed observer without notice and injuring her in the process.

Lee takes one look and leaves to find somewhere else to sleep. The others stay, and the clerks immediately record Bo's welcome as consent to hold the proceedings in his house.

Summer wants three things:

- A replacement radio, plastic-cased, equal or better quality.
- His apartment made available to the observer and up to eight attendants for three sunsets and the nights between them. No deliberately introduced iron.
- An acknowledgement that he ended a diplomatic presence without warning or a chance to talk.

Bo wants her to specify **Material Plane sunsets**, on the record. Celadine tries to turn his willingness to replace the radio into an admission of guilt. He agrees the radio was damaged and refuses to concede anything beyond that.

Pierre tells him to stop talking and get a fucking lawyer. Celadine says counsel must be licensed in the Summer Court. Bo doesn't qualify for a public defender because he isn't Fey.

Celadine also has her own Fifth Amendment. The accused gets to answer without interruption. If he refuses to answer, the accusation is accepted as true.

> "That's bullshit."
>
> Pierre

Chandler challenges her jurisdiction over an apartment in South Boston, Massachusetts. Celadine claims the land belonged to the Fey first, including the fen beneath Fenway. She continues the hearing.

### The Other Offer

Celadine offers Bo the proof he's spent years trying to get anyone to take seriously: a classification for his mycelium, a way to show its retained impressions, a repeatable laboratory test, and a Harvard scholar to verify the results.

In return, he and any friends who agree must stop investigating Summer's work. They also can't record it, interfere with it, tell anyone about it, or warn people about it until the current emergency is over. She doesn't define what counts as Summer's work.

Before anyone accepts, somebody knocks at the door.

## Part Two: He's My Lawyer Now

[[NPCs/Harvard/Dr. Richard Sparks\|Dr. Richard Sparks]] is standing outside in tweed, a waistcoat, and a bow tie. He comes in and tells Bo to decline the offer. Celadine knows him and is immediately unhappy.

> "Nope, he's hired. He's my lawyer now."
>
> Bo

Sparks asks who can act under Summer's authority and whether it can claim something after it has happened. He also wants to know whether warning somebody or helping an injured person would violate the agreement, and what activating the mycelium would do to Bo.

Celadine stalls, adjourns the hearing, and disappears. The clerks walk behind the jars and vanish, leaving their office furniture on the counter.

Sparks is a history professor at Harvard. He says keeping track of the city's supernatural problems is part of his job. He points out that Bo **had asked the fairy to leave**, repeatedly, and should have said so during the hearing. Sparks expects Summer to return with a different accusation.

Chandler shows him Kelsey's drawing. Sparks recognizes someone from Winter and gets concerned enough to say he needs to speak with Leonard and Edwin. He gives Bo his Harvard card and heads out. Bo tries to work out what he felt around Sparks. He got no Summer or Winter stink, just the safety of being at his grandparents' house.

## Part Three: A Mushroom on a Chain

The party heads for Jacques's suite at [[Locations/Park Plaza Hotel\|Park Plaza Hotel]]. Chandler makes a stop first: $60 for a bottle of Midleton Very Rare 1984, dropped at the precinct for [[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]. The desk officer says O'Malley has called in all his sick time. He'll get the bottle whenever he comes back.

At the hotel, Pierre brings out the cold pencil shard from the crime scene. Bo examines it through his jacket cuff and puts it in a sample container. The material resembles ice that won't melt; there's no wood or graphite.

When it gets close to Bo's **Luminous Hollow sample**, the fungus lights up and the jar vibrates. Moving the two back and forth establishes a range of **ten feet**.

Bo wants to wear the sample somewhere he'll notice it going off. After a discussion about whether he is asking Chandler to hand over his dog tags, they put the specimen on Chandler's chain instead. Chandler wears it under his shirt. The shard gets brought close again, and the necklace glows, warms up, and buzzes against his chest.

They call it [[Campaign Notes/FADAR\|FADAR]] and agree on a signal when it goes off: **"Does anything here remind you of home?"**

They have tested it against the shard, so far. Bo hopes it will detect other Fey, but points out that carrying the shard beside it would turn their detector into a flashlight. They still need somewhere to keep the shard.

Jacques brings up **Boston Garden**. It's in the middle of the mapped incidents, and there's a rink full of ice. He can get the others tickets for November 15, then try to stay after the game and look around. Everyone gets some sleep. Overnight counts as a short rest; a long rest is still a week.

## Part Four: Shopping Takes All Day

**Thursday, 7:38 a.m.** [[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Harry Sinden\|Harry Sinden]] calls the suite. Jacques tells Harry he lost his pager, and Harry arranges a replacement.

Jacques asks for the **same number** as the pager they threw out because Bo gave it to Kelsey. Jacques is relying on O'Malley's promise to drop the investigation, despite the detective now being on sick leave.

Harry arranges friends' tickets for the 15th, docking Jacques's paycheck $20. If Jacques wants the rink after hours, he needs to talk to [[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Frank Galloway\|Frank Galloway]], night security.

After making fun of Bo for buying Flintstones vitamins, the others buy some themselves during the pharmacy stop.

### Kenmore Army Navy

Jacques drives Bo and Chandler the 1.86 miles to [[Locations/Kenmore Army Navy\|Kenmore Army Navy]]. Rush hour keeps them in the Mustang for two hours.

[[NPCs/Kenmore Army Navy/Ned Kenmore\|Ned Kenmore]] sells knives, protective gear, and field supplies. Chandler wants iron weapons. Ned brings out Northern Fleet Service Bayonets while the two trade military service stories. Their effectiveness against Fey is still untested.

Jacques buys Kevlar for himself and Leonard because somebody needs to keep the kid alive. He can't stack it with his hockey armor. Bo buys a first-aid kit, Neosporin, and a flashlight, along with other supplies.

Ned has no arrows for Jacques's bow.

### Pierre Was Left Unsupervised

Back at the suite, Leonard has barely touched his rum and Coke. Pierre has gone through most of the bottle.

Jacques takes Pierre to the sink and dunks his face in cold water. [[NPCs/Boston Harbor/Trudy\|Trudy]] forces her way into the room and starts bumping Jacques to get him off her rider. Pierre has to mentally tell his motorcycle that it's fine, they're friends, nobody is hurting him.

Leonard admits he only accepted the drink to be polite, so Bo takes it off him. Jacques gives Leonard the Kevlar vest.

### Chandler Gets Paid

Chandler decides to take his article to [[Locations/The Boston Globe\|The Boston Globe]] himself so he can get paid today. The hotel has a fax, but a fax won't get him a check before the bank closes.

His editor approves the refrigeration-accident story because Chandler has supplied names, ages, a location, and the official explanation without mentioning any flying saucers. He keeps the line about one victim thinking it's 1974.

A **20 on Performance** gets Chandler **80 cents a word: $123.20 for 154 words.** Chandler gets his check, stops at the bank, and comes back around half past noon to find Pierre hungover and a motorcycle in the suite. He makes coffee.

### Still No Arrows

They call Sparks about the Grief-Eater's last words. His answering machine has an entire clause explaining that leaving a message does not constitute invitation, hospitality, a compact, surrender, or consent. They decide against putting the question on tape and go to Harvard.

They stop at the gun store first, where Jacques produces his longbow and asks about arrows. The owner is impressed that he hunts moose with it, but has no suitable arrows either.

Chandler buys **$10 of .38 Special ammunition**. They take the Red Line to Harvard.

Sparks isn't in his Robinson Hall office. His schedule is mostly field trips, and Thursday's entry is missing. Friday's is there: Boston Stone at 12:30, Blackstone Block at 1:15, Custom House steps at 2:30, Long Wharf at 3:15. They'll catch him tomorrow.

## Part Five: Boston Jesus

Pierre calls the bakery. The woman who answers lowers her voice: men in leather jackets have been coming by almost every day. He tells her not to give away where he is. Going home is still a bad idea.

On Friday, the group tries to work out which Custom House Sparks means. Bo gets a **natural 20 on History** and directs them to the old site by [[Locations/Old State House\|Old State House]].

Chandler has Detect Magic running when Sparks arrives with twelve students. Sparks is so bright it hurts to look at him. Chandler drops concentration and spends **the rest of November 14 with disadvantage on Perception**.

They let the lecture finish. Sparks sends the students off for lunch, turns around, and asks what they want.

### The Grief-Eater's Last Words

Bo reads him the message:

> "The harvest was denied. The courts remember. Winter judges. Summer rages. The green one dreams of walking."

Sparks checks the wording and asks how the creature sounded when it died. The party remembers relief. He tells them to find out what was being cultivated, when it was supposed to be collected, and who would benefit. They still don't know what the creature intended to do with its captives.

If Summer comes back demanding reparations, he suggests asking whether the creature was its property, its tool, its responsibility, or an opportunity the party ruined.

Bo asks why they were all drawn to Spectacle Island in the first place. Sparks says **Boston is alive**. It brought them together to help its inhabitants. He is surprised this is the first he's heard about their trip to the island; normally he or one of his two companions would know.

Sparks refuses to identify his two companions. When pressed about himself, he insists they keep treating him as an old history professor. He says dropping that pretense would unravel the magic holding Boston together and kill millions.

Pierre calls him "Boston Jesus." Sparks accepts it.

### Bo Has Had Enough

Sparks glances west at the mention of the Green One. He wants to investigate his suspicions before sending the party somewhere dangerous. He warns that "walking" could mean an advancing boundary or something spreading, as well as a creature moving.

Bo tells him they need somewhere to start looking.

> "We're not going to stand by and let people die. And we're definitely going to get the fucking Faye out of my apartment."
>
> Bo

Sparks finally explains what he's doing. He and his companions are getting old. If he solves everything himself, nobody learns how to take over and he never gets to retire. He'll help with things beyond them, but they need to handle the things they can.

Chandler tells him about the mapped cold incidents and the Garden theory. Sparks says to start at the Garden. He takes Jacques's pager number and agrees to call if his research produces something useful. He also clarifies that Bo announcing he's hired does not mean they have a standing lawyer. He has other obligations and can't promise he'll be there next time Summer shows up.

### The Pencil Belongs to Mab's Scout

Pierre reminds Bo to show him the shard. This time Sparks examines it properly.

It belongs to the figure in Kelsey's drawing. It's a writing instrument, and it can leave the frost-script they've been finding. The figure is **the Scout**, Queen Mab's foremost scout. He looks for distress and sorrow. Winter feeds on that.

Chandler shows Diggs's Polaroid from the monument. Sparks recognizes the writing as a date connected to the Sox losing the Series. The exact date needs checking; the one agreed at the table doesn't match the existing timeline.

Chandler thanks Sparks for the information, and Jacques accidentally calls him "coach" as he goes back to his students. Bo has already walked away.

## Next Session

At wrap-up, the GM tells Chandler what he finds when he goes to pick up Ruth Hanlon for their date: **a for-sale sign in front of the house**. Chandler wants to check the obituaries. That search hasn't happened, and nobody has confirmed what happened to Ruth.

The party is ready to move ahead to **the November 15 game at [[Locations/Boston Garden\|Boston Garden]]**. They still need Frank's permission to stay after hours. The rink hasn't been investigated.

Other business still hanging:

- Celadine adjourned the hearing. Bo hasn't settled anything with Summer.
- FADAR needs testing against something other than the shard, and the shard needs to stay away from the necklace.
- Sparks is looking into the Green One. He hasn't named it.
- The harvest is still unexplained.
- Men are watching Pierre's bakery flat.
- O'Malley is on sick leave. The bottle is waiting at the precinct.
