# Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee?

> [!info] Session Details
> **Date Played:** October 25, 2026
> **In-Game Dates:** Friday afternoon, November 14, through Saturday, November 15, 1986, before the game.
> **Locations:** Chandler's apartment, [[Locations/The Plough & Stars\|The Plough & Stars]], Ruth Hanlon's house in Charlestown, [[Locations/Warren Tavern\|Warren Tavern]], [[Locations/Bull and Finch Pub\|Bull and Finch Pub]], O'Malley's home, Boston Garden, and an alley where Pierre practices magic with Bo.
> **Players:** [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler T. Harrow]]; [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]]; [[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]]; [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]; [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]. Young is absent.
> **Previous:** [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]]

Chandler tries to go on his date. The others follow him, get caught, and spend the evening following him anyway. He takes them to Cambridge by mistake, which gets an actual investigation done before anyone notices. While four of them eat dinner, Chandler climbs through Ruth Hanlon's bathroom window and finds blood on the carpet. When he comes back to explain, Leonard and Jacques have no idea who Lee is.

---

## Part One: Everyone Is Following Chandler

It's Friday afternoon. Sparks has gone back to his students, Bo is grinding his teeth, and Chandler needs to get ready for his promised drink with [[NPCs/Charlestown/Ruth Hanlon\|Ruth Hanlon]]. He heads home. The other four decide to tail him from a block away.

**Group Stealth: 7.** Leonard gets a 20, but the others ruin it. Chandler stops for a cigarette and sees the entire group pop around a corner, then try to hide again. He ducks aside, lets them walk past, and answers Jacques when he asks why they're all following him.

Chandler doesn't particularly want the date, but he gave Ruth his word. He wouldn't object if somebody interrupted with an urgent reason for him to leave. Jacques offers Drakkar Noir, has to repeat the name several times, then sprays him with it.

> "So I smell like Pine Sol, in other words."
>
> Chandler

At the apartment, Chandler puts on *Everybody Knows* and gets in the shower to wash it off. Pierre leaves Trudy outside and promises not to break any more furniture. The coffee table, Chandler tells him, was fifteen bucks from a thrift shop.

### Bo Wants to Know What They're Doing

Bo looks over Chandler's investigation board while the others settle in. They've pissed off two courts, nearly died, and still don't know what they're trying to accomplish. He believes something magically drew him to Spectacle Island, and he's tired of being pulled into things by people who won't explain them.

Pierre has been improvising since someone tried to drown him. He'll take this over being dead. Leonard wants to study what's happening, but knows he needs help if it turns violent. Chandler calls from the shower that he's trying to put the ghosts of his past to rest, literal and otherwise.

Jacques brings them back to the Garden. They have tickets for tomorrow. The rink is cold, it's in the middle of the mapped incidents, and he wants to find out what's there. Bo agrees. His time off runs out on November 20, and he wants answers while they can still work together.

## Part Two: The Plough & Stars

Chandler dresses, and the group takes the T to Cambridge. They have time before the date, so he looks for a nearby bar. [[Locations/The Plough & Stars\|The Plough & Stars]] is up the street at 912 Massachusetts Avenue. It's also one of the cold-incident sites they never visited.

Bo is pleased they're finally checking something off the list. Chandler whistles *Somebody's Watching Me* for his Detect Magic ritual on the walk over.

### Four Seats and a Wiped Mirror

Inside, Chandler detects **Divination and Enchantment** at four places along the bar. It matches the combination at the monument. The mirror behind the bar holds **three patches of Evocation**, with weaker traces on the taps. Whatever was written on the glass has been wiped away. He can't recover the symbols.

[[NPCs/The Plough & Stars/Seamus Connolly\|Seamus Connolly]], the owner, notices him studying the mirror. Chandler orders a Sam Adams and asks about the night the patrons froze. Leonard buys a Tab. Bo buys the $6.95 ribeye and steak fries. Jacques asks for the strongest beer and ends up with a Guinness containing a shot of Jameson, Baileys, and Kahlua. Pierre gets a warm bottle of Labatt Blue and tries to look grateful.

Seamus says he left [[NPCs/Cambridge/Dennis Fahey\|Dennis Fahey]] watching the bar while he took a smoke break. When he returned, four men were frozen in their seats, their drinks and the taps frozen solid, despite the heat being set to 68 degrees. He puts it somewhere between ten and midnight and says the police arrived shortly after he called.

Chandler has the report. It says Seamus closed at 2 a.m., returned at 7, and found the men still there. The police and ambulance came that morning. Those accounts don't match.

### Seamus Remembers Remembering

Chandler can tell Seamus is holding something back. He takes a cough drop for the Persuasion bonus, rolls a 13, and spends **two Tenacity** to reach the DC 15.

Seamus admits his memory of that whole night is getting worse. He normally remembers everything. He knows he remembered the details when he first spoke to the police. Later, while cleaning, he wiped frost markings off the mirror and felt something happen. Since then, that night's events have become harder to recall. Everything else is fine.

> "I remember remembering."
>
> Seamus Connolly

He asks Chandler to keep him out of anything he prints. Fahey hasn't been back. Seamus remembers an old childhood address on Ellery Street, possibly number 150, but warns that it's thirty years out of date.

### Bob, Spelled B-O-H-B

Bo slides four Flintstones vitamins across the bar to help Seamus if the Fey return. Asked his name, he says Bob. B-O-H-B.

Seamus has heard about a large, brown-haired, bearded man handing vitamins to children. He looks at the D.A.R.E. sticker on the wall.

Chandler tries to explain the iron content and why it helped against things they've encountered. Leonard supplies the chemistry. Seamus remains unconvinced, pushes the vitamins back, and shows them the revolver he keeps under the bar.

Chandler leaves $20 against a $10 drinks tab. Outside, Bo insists he knows what he's doing. Jacques supports the vitamin plan. Leonard asks him to tone it down.

## Part Three: Wrong Side of Town

Chandler picks Casablanca on Brattle Street for dinner, then checks Ruth's address. **Cordis Street. Charlestown. By Bunker Hill.** They've spent the evening in Cambridge because he mixed two case locations together.

> "And that's called manifestation, baby."
>
> Bo

They take the T back to Arlington, paying **$3.15 each** for the trip, then collect Jacques's Mustang at the Park Plaza. Jacques asks why they're going to so much trouble for a date Chandler doesn't want. Chandler points out that he never asked any of them to come.

On the way, Leonard says Diane is still unconscious as far as he knows. Chandler describes the magic he saw in the Plough and asks whether the others recognize anything that could explain Seamus's missing memories. They don't settle on an answer.

Once they're in Charlestown, Chandler still needs a restaurant. After briefly considering Papa Gino's, he chooses [[Locations/Warren Tavern\|Warren Tavern]] at 2 Pleasant Street. He gets out near Cordis Street and tells the others to make themselves scarce. They go to the tavern ahead of him.

## Part Four: Dinner for Four, Break-In for One

The four get a booth. The [[NPCs/Warren Tavern/Warren Tavern Waitress\|waitress]] takes their orders while Bo works out how to spy on the date as a spider.

Leonard gets a burger without pickles, well-done fries, and a Coke. Pierre orders chowder, haddock with mashed turnip, and a bottled Budweiser. Jacques gets chowder, Narragansett, and shepherd's pie after trying to order it as *pate chinois*, which starts an argument about the corn. They get potato skins with Swiss for the table.

Bo orders three whiskey sours. Four arrive, and he drinks them before attempting the transformation. He's already eaten at the other bar.

![Session 06 - Warren Tavern Dinner.jpg|Dinner at the Warren Tavern|700](/img/user/Images/Sessions/Session%2006%20-%20Warren%20Tavern%20Dinner.jpg)

### Ruth Isn't Home

Chandler reaches Ruth's house and finds it looking abandoned, with a for-sale sign out front. The Careys' house is still occupied. Detect Magic picks up **Enchantment** through the obscured window in Ruth's front door.

He checks the street and doesn't spot anyone watching. At **7:08 p.m.**, he checks her mailbox: two days' worth of envelopes addressed to Ruth. He leaves the mail there.

There are no signs of forced entry, but closer investigation reveals **Conjuration in the shape of footsteps** on the front stoop. The front and back doors are locked. The kitchen window is locked. The bathroom window opens.

Chandler climbs in. He uses a handkerchief to avoid fingerprints, fumbles it on the bathroom doorknob, and wipes the knob down before going farther.

### The Living Room

The furniture is still there. A dark red stain lies on the carpet, with blood on the edge of the coffee table and a police evidence tag beside it.

**Investigation 24**, including the magnifying-glass bonus: someone was startled, turned quickly, tripped, and struck their head on the table. Chandler assumes it was Ruth, but can't confirm whose blood it is or whether the person survived.

A second pair of Conjuration footprints stands beyond the stain. They match the pair outside and the footprints at the monument. Chandler concludes the Scout has been here.

In the kitchen, he finds her late husband's urn on the refrigerator and a bridge-club contact number attached to it. There are no apparent family contacts. He copies the number, leaves through the bathroom window, and heads for the tavern. He hasn't found Ruth, a body, or anything confirming her death.

### Bo's Timing

Bo takes Jacques into the alley for the transformation. His bones crack and his skeleton moves outside his body, with a yelp and most of the whiskey sours coming back up before his human face disappears. He ends as a small spider standing in his own vomit. Jacques nearly throws up helping him onto his shirt.

They head back toward the entrance and meet Chandler coming the other way, alone.

> "It's gonna be dinner for one."
>
> Chandler

Bo drops Wild Shape, shoves off Jacques's back as he grows, and complains that Chandler could have arrived two minutes earlier. Chandler tells them there's a good chance Ruth is dead and that he thinks their friend Jack Frost was involved.

They go inside. Chandler takes a potato skin and explains what he found while the others are eating.

## Part Five: Who the Fuck Is Lee?

Chandler says he tried to be sneaky, but he isn't Lee. **Leonard and Jacques don't know who he's talking about.**

At first it sounds like a joke about Lee's various names. Chandler explains the man who came to the island with Pierre, stayed at the hotel, and got them into trouble there. Pierre remembers him. Bo remembers him. Leonard counts the party and gets five.

> "You keep on thinking Lee, Lee, Lee, like we know who Lee is. Who the fuck is Lee?"
>
> Jacques

Bo checks their faces. They have **no recognition at all**. They aren't fucking with anyone. Leonard and Jacques genuinely don't remember Lee.

Chandler writes everyone's names and descriptions in his notebook, in Vietnamese. They decide to take a group photo, then decide against doing it here, a block from the house Chandler just broke into. They head for [[Locations/Bull and Finch Pub\|the Bull & Finch]] instead. Chandler also wants a record of being somewhere else tonight.

Jacques drives back to the Park Plaza. Friday traffic, parking, and the walk put them at the pub around **9:30 p.m.** Chandler buys six Jamesons, including one for the person taking the picture, for **$3**. They get a photograph of all five present, dated **November 14, 1986, 9:37 p.m.**

![Session 06 - Bull and Finch Group Photo.jpg|The five present at the Bull and Finch, November 14, 1986, 9:37 p.m.|700](/img/user/Images/Sessions/Session%2006%20-%20Bull%20and%20Finch%20Group%20Photo.jpg)

Lee is still missing from the group. The photo records who is here tonight; it hasn't brought back anyone's memory.

## Part Six: Saturday Before the Game

The group sleeps. The GM removes the short-rest limit that was preventing nightly recovery. **They can take an overnight short rest each day. A long rest still requires a week of downtime.**

They haven't recovered everything. Chandler is down two first-level spell slots, one second-level slot, and Bardic Inspiration. Leonard has used his free Eldritch Cannon and is missing a spell slot. Bo has his Wild Shapes back. Pierre is also short on spell slots.

### O'Malley Is Missing

Chandler tries [[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]] again. The precinct says emergency leave, no check-in. His home phone rings unanswered.

Chandler goes to the house and finds [[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Maureen O'Malley\|Maureen and the children]] outside in a wood-paneled station wagon. They've come to check on him too. Nobody answers the door. His usual bars either haven't seen him or won't say whether they have.

Maureen and Chandler exchange contact information and agree to tell each other if they hear anything. Chandler promises to start looking for him.

### Frank Will Let Them Stay

Jacques meets [[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Frank Galloway\|Frank Gallo]], the Garden security guard he knows. Frank is 58, has worked there for 22 years, and thinks Jacques did his job in the incident that got him suspended.

He trusts Jacques. He wants to know why four friends need to stay after closing, particularly when one is a reporter. Jacques says he wants to get back on the ice with friends and remember playing hockey.

The Persuasion check works. They already have tickets. When the crowd leaves, **all five are to report to the security desk**. Frank will take them to a back room. They wait there for **an hour and a half**, then meet him by the rink shield. Once everything has cleared, he'll let them onto the ice.

The game is scheduled for **7:35-10:15 p.m.** They're to arrive around seven. That gives them the day to prepare.

### Pierre Asks for Help

Pierre visits Bo, who is finally clearing Summer's furniture out of his apartment. He offers to help, then asks about magic. The last time he tried something, Chandler's table exploded. He'd like more control over it.

They find an alley to practice. Bo shows him the little sprig he uses as a focus, taken from the tree above the Hollow, then casts **Shillelagh on his machete**. It glows green. He explains how the focus helps him direct the magic he already has and asks Pierre whether anything connects him to that part of himself.

Pierre takes out his motorcycle-club patch. Those people are his family. He focuses on it and points at the rubbish.

A bell sounds. Six rats scatter. One dies to **Toll the Dead**, clutching its head before it bursts. Pierre tries again and casts **Eldritch Blast**, disintegrating another rat.

He gets excited explaining how he thinks it works. Dark tendrils rise around both men's feet as he starts casting **Arms of Hadar**. Bo backs away. Pierre catches it and can stop before completing the spell or spending a slot.

> "Did you just summon the devil? What the fuck was that?"
>
> Bo

The session ends in the alley, before anyone goes to the hockey game.
