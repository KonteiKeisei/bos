# Session 04 — Roger G-Man

> [!info] Session Details
> **In-Game Date:** November 12, 1986 (evening, continuing from Session 03)
> **Location:** Bo's apartment → Charlestown (14 Cordis Street & 12 Cordis Street) → Café Pamplona, Cambridge → Site 1 (Long Wharf–adjacent alley) → Bo's apartment again
> **System:** Urban Arcana / D&D 5e Homebrew
> **Players:** [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler T. Harrow]] · [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]] · [[Player Characters/Lee Smith\|Lee]] · [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] · [[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]] · [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] — full party

Six people who now agree that magic is real and Boston is haunted spend an afternoon doing detective work in Charlestown, an evening interviewing a Harvard consultant at a Cambridge café, a night fighting a Winter Wolf at Long Wharf, and the last five minutes of the session opening the door on [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]'s apartment to find the Summer Court has moved in. Along the way, [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] hands a 17-year-old girl a bottle of iron-content Flintstones vitamins, gives her [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]'s pager number under the alias **Roger G-Man**, and starts a chain of events that ends with [[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]] sitting on the ground with his service weapon drawn, drinking from a flask, having his mind read against his will, and then quietly deciding to take a vacation.

The rest of the evening is a lot.

## Part One: The Careys of Charlestown

**14 Cordis Street, 5:50 PM.** Two cars in the driveway — a beige Toyota sedan and a black BMW. Fifty-nine degrees, warm for mid-November. Streetlights not quite on yet.

Assignments:
- [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] on the knock, press pass ready
- [[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]] behind him with a notepad
- [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] cannot come inside — he has a home game (allegedly)
- [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] and [[Player Characters/Lee Smith\|Lee]] across the street
- [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]] parallel to the BMW

### Robert Carey Deflects

[[NPCs/Charlestown/Robert Carey\|Robert Carey]] opens the door. Big guy, quiet, tired. Chandler shows the Globe credential and gets exactly one useful thing out of him.

> *"Sandra, honey, this is more your thing than my thing. Just deal with them for the next five minutes and I will take over from there. Let me finish what it is that I am working on."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Robert Carey\|Robert Carey]]**

Robert lets them in, then retreats to the kitchen to swear at his Salisbury steak gravy and mashed potatoes for the rest of the interview.

Cold came from the corner where the TV was. Family was watching a Game 7 recap on Channel 4. Yes, they'd all been under. **Chandler asks whether anyone else was in the house. Robert stops the conversation.**

> *"I don't remember telling you I saw something."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Robert Carey\|Robert Carey]]**

Persuasion 5. Chandler is dismissed.

### Detect Magic in the Living Room

[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] starts casting Detect Magic as a ritual — GM immediately notes the verbal and somatic components. Ten minutes of *"aaaaahhhhh"* and hand-wiggling in the Careys' living room is discussed briefly, aborted. [[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]] taps his shoulder and offers to burn a spell slot on his **Ecto Arcane Scanner** instead, which Robert grants because Leonard looks 15 and harmless.

The scanner starts beep-blooping. Robert notices. It becomes background.

### Kelsey Carey

[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]] rounds the corner mid-conversation — 17, sharp, plays D&D, has been trolling her hair dreamily since she saw the entity in her living room. She shakes hands with Chandler firmly. Talks debate club, extracurriculars.

She waits for her father to leave the room. Then she gives Chandler what he came for.

> *"Listen. We all saw what we saw. But Ma keeps calling Dad crazy, so he doesn't want to talk about it. He's sensitive about anything that Mom cares about. So he ain't gonna tell you shit."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]]**

Household economic tension: Sandra outearns Robert. Robert grounded Kelsey for two days after the incident, assuming she was drugged.

> *"Well, we all saw it. I know we did, because I heard them talking about it, but they didn't see it like I saw it. I didn't go all the way under. I kept coming back to. And I saw it. Standing by the TV. It was too tall. Made out of cold. Felt like it was writing something down."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]]**

She pulls a notebook out of her school bag. Multiple drawings of the entity — later described by Chandler as *"Jack Frost and Edward from Twilight had a baby and it knew how to act."* She gives Chandler one of the pages.

**Key data point:**

> *"I felt like it was draining, like my emotions. I definitely felt things. I just didn't feel the things that I wanted to feel."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]]**

> *"What did you feel when Buckner missed that ball?"*
> — **[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]**

> *"Oh, I don't give a shit. That's baseball. That's dad stuff."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]]**

She kept coming to *because she wasn't invested.* The entity was after her parents' grief, not hers.

### Sandra Carey

After Kelsey heads back to the kitchen, [[NPCs/Charlestown/Sandra Carey\|Sandra Carey]] joins Chandler. She's finishing paperwork for tomorrow's shift — Chandler notices a badge on her lapel:

> [!note] Sandra's employer
> **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Sandra Carey\|Sandra Carey]] is Assistant Manager at the front desk of Spalding Rehabilitation Hospital.** She hands Chandler her business card unprompted. In 1986 Boston that's a solidly upper-middle-class institutional job — she outearns her husband, and she knows it.

Corroborates Robert's account of the cold. Estimates the temperature dropped to about 33–34°F, coming specifically from the corner. Comes to with Kelsey across the room with a *"dreamy look on her face."* Grounded her.

Then she looks past Chandler and asks Leonard a question that stops the whole conversation cold:

> *"What are you getting on that thing? Is that an oscillating EM scanner he's got there?"*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Sandra Carey\|Sandra Carey]]**

She named it correctly. Leonard confirms. She takes the answer at face value. Chandler moves the interview on.


Chandler thanks the family. Leaves with Kelsey's drawing.

## Part Three: Café Pamplona

12 Bow Street, Cambridge. The party arrives about 11 minutes early. It has started to drizzle.

Behind the counter: **[[NPCs/Café Pamplona/Elisa\|Elisa]]** — Italian, dark hair, warm and cutting in the same breath. She insults the party into hospitality throughout the entire visit.

Orders:
- [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] — Colombian coffee, black
- [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] — black coffee (won't drink it; warms his hands with it)
- [[Player Characters/Lee Smith\|Lee]] — water
- [[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]] — tall cold glass of milk and an oatmeal-raisin cookie the size of his face
- [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]] — coffee with two sugars, no cream
- [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] *(arrives late)* — latte and a chocolate croissant

$5.75 total, Chandler pays with a six-dollar tip.

[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] attempts a **Sleight of Hand DC 10** to steal a piece of Leonard's cookie. **Fails.** Elisa yanks the entire cookie out of his reach at speed.

> *"You know, in my line of business, I hear like, taking candy from a baby, but what the fuck, Chandler?"*
> — **[[Player Characters/Lee Smith\|Lee]]**

### The Osei Interview

**[[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]]** — sitting in a corner, having declined to speak until Chandler was seated — corrects him on his form of address:

> *"That's Doctor, I'm saying."*
> — **[[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]]**

Her actual role: **Associate Professor for Urban Planning at RBU, with consultant work for Harvard on ancient script.** Multiple published studies.

She hands over a full written statement rather than being interviewed cold — because she's an academic and *"I write things down immediately when they're unusual."*

> *"The boundary of cold was abrupt, not a patch. I stopped because it felt like walking into a wall of refrigerated air. I estimated 25–30 degrees differential over less than two feet, which is not normal. The frost on the monument base was organized, structured, and directional. I did not recognize the script. It appeared quite abruptly. I had a sense that I had lost some time. After looking at my watch, it appears that I lost about five minutes. I have working knowledge of Latin, French, and some classical Greek, and it was none of those."*
> — **[[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]]** *(from her written statement)*

> *"I was there for approximately 90 seconds. During that time I felt observed. Something in that space was aware of my presence. Are you familiar with the seventh sense, the sense of being watched, a thing that we are just now beginning to uncover as part of our genetic diversity and evolutionary path? It is real. I could feel it. Like I was prey and there was a predator in the area. I was peripheral to whatever it was doing. I then left. I am not embarrassed that I left."*
> — **[[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]]**

The party learns three things they didn't have before:

1. **The script has continued to appear.** On the morning of November 10, Patricia returned to the monument and saw a **fourth grouping of writing — larger than the previous three.** Not present when she arrived that morning. It formed before she got there.
2. **She has counted the characters.** She doesn't recognize the script, but she knows how much of it there is.
3. **Her framing of the event is judicial.**

> *"The quality of it, it feels like evidence is being gathered."*
> — **[[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]]**

> *"Almost like an investigation."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]**

> *"Eh, yes. Or a trial."*
> — **[[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]]**

That last exchange lands hard for anyone at the table who remembers the Grief-Eater's last words on Spectacle Island: *"The harvest was denied. The courts will remember. Winter judges. Summer rages. The Green One dreams of walking."*

Chandler runs **Insight** — she is being entirely forthcoming within her knowledge. She has no observation to add about a figure, because she never saw one. She also declines a follow-up dinner and leaves with a "Watson out" once she's finished her lobster-tail pastry.


Chandler gives her his card. She raises an eyebrow — "Dick Tracy" — is unimpressed by "Clark Kent" and only slightly less so by "Bob Woodward." She observes the "entourage" as she leaves.

> *"Sounds like an interesting puzzle."*
> — **[[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]]**

I don't know nothing. I just saw spooky lights from across the street. I think that they're all playing that satanic panic game."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Ruth Hanlon\|Ruth Hanlon]]**

> *"Dungeons and Dragons."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]**

> *"Diddlers and doodads, that's the one. Yeah, they're Satan worshippers, and that's what's happening here."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Ruth Hanlon\|Ruth Hanlon]]**

She then slams the door. Friday-drink appointment stands.

## Part Five: Roger G-Man

The party debates: is [[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]] awakened, or was her resistance to the entity's Enchantment because she was emotionally neutral toward the game? [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] proposes a test — send someone she hasn't seen and see if she recognizes them for what they are.

[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] volunteers.

He puts on his City of Boston Sanitation Department hat, crosses the street, and knocks on the Careys' door.

Kelsey answers.

> *"Hi, what's up? What can I do for you?"*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]]**

> *"My name is Roger, and I am… what am I doing here?"*
> — **[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]** *(out loud, panicking)*

> *"Yeah, the city is doing a youth recycling project, and your school said that you might be interested."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]**

Kelsey pushes back on Bo's pitch. Points out that Boston is *made* of landfill. Asks for a pamphlet. Bo doesn't have one.

> *"I have done a poor job of selling me this program."*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]]**

Bo pivots to the pager exchange. She writes "Roger" on his card and asks for his last name.

> *"G-Man."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]**

Kelsey writes it down. **Bo's rolls this scene are consistently near or at nat 1.** Persuasion, deception, performance — all sitting at −1. The scene is a full-body failure. Bo doubles down.

He pulls out an unlabeled white bottle of **Flintstones Chewable Vitamins** (three of them), removed from its packaging because *"I don't like the feel of the wrapper."*

> *"These will keep you safe from the devil."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]**

> *"Are these drugs?"*
> — **[[NPCs/Charlestown/Kelsey Carey\|Kelsey Carey]]**

> *"Yes. Yes."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]**

Bo writes down [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]'s pager number as his callback. Kelsey files it. Bo departs.

Distant police sirens.

The party bolts to the car. Bo cheerfully explains what he did.

> *"Well, assuming they can track who owns the number I gave that 17-year-old girl, you should get a new pager."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]** *(to Jacques)*

[[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] refuses to throw his team-issued pager out the window himself. [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]] does it for him.

> *"Oh, sacrament."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]**

Jacques will need to report it stolen — allegedly stolen by *"Robert G-Man."*

## Part Six: Site 1 — The Long Wharf Alley

The party drives to the alley where [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] first found [[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]] and [[NPCs/Boston Paranormal Society/Diane Okoye\|Diane]] on October 27. 11:45 PM. Drizzle.

### Chandler Plays the Flute

[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] begins casting **Detect Magic as a ritual.** For his verbal/somatic component, he pulls out the classical silver flute Sister Margaret gave him at nine years old — the one that's been through Vietnam with him and hasn't been touched in decades — and plays **"For What It's Worth"** for the full ten minutes.

The rest of the party stares.

### Forensics

Party splits investigations:

- **Chandler + Leonard + Jacques — Arcana (group check):** Leonard rolls a **nat 20** with two helpers.
  - The residual energy on the sidewalk is **100% Fey in origin. Winter Court.**
  - This was *not* an attack site. The two victims were in the path of *something moving with concentration toward something else.* They interfered. They were dealt with defensively.
- **Bo — Nature with advantage:** Rolls a **nat 20** for a 25 total.
  - The energy taxonomy is the same wavelength as the dark growth at Blount Fisheries — not identical, but *related the way sharks and fish are related.*
  - The paw print is a **Winter Wolf.**
- **Pierre — Religion:** Rolls high.
  - His grandmother's stories about the **Frost Wolf** — Unseelie Court spirit, creature of cold and hatred. Stalks its prey. If you cross its path — prey or not — it will freeze you and take your soul to the Nine Hells.
- **Lee + Jacques — Perception on the crime scene:**
  - Nothing lines up with the police report. **There is one more chalk outline than there were victims.** *The third body has not been accounted for.*
  - An **old woman is watching from a window across the street.** She sees the party looking and closes her blinds.

Chandler picks up a **newspaper page** on the ground — rehashing thoughts on Buckner's fumble, whether he was paid off, his actual excellent career until that point.

Pierre finds, nestled in blades of grass, what looks at first like a black carpenter's pencil. He picks it up. It **burns his fingers** — not from heat but from cold. **A shard of winter shaped like a pencil.** Takes 1 damage.

And then Leonard and Bo, together, look at the **arcane sigil** on the ground between the chalk figures.

> *"If you're just looking, you're going to roll an Arcana with disadvantage. If you choose to interact with it — kneel down, place your hands on it, touch it — you will have advantage on the roll. It is your choice."*
> — **GM**

Bo touches it. Leonard touches it. Bo spends all of his tenacity to bump the roll to a **20.**

> [!important] What the sigil is
> The sigil is the **final word in a Fey inscription that was originally a record, not a ward or a trap.** It has been made *intentionally visible* by whoever left it. That intentional visibility is what makes it a trap.

Whoever left it now knows they were interfered with.

## Part Seven: The Winter Wolf

Out of the mist coalesces a wolf the size of a horse. **Winter Wolf.** Initiative rolled.

### Combat Highlights

**Round 1**

**[[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]]** throws down his **Eldritch Cannon** (Spectral Anomaly Neutralizer — his in-fiction rebranding of the Force Ballista) and fires. Hit for 5 damage.

**[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]** moves to close the 30-foot range gap on **Hideous Laughter,** casts it on the wolf.

> *"Now we find out if this wolf understands a meta-future Bill Cosby reference."*
> — **GM**

> *"Big Bad Wolf. He can't be any worse than Bo's attempt at picking up an underage child earlier."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]**

Wolf saves. Chandler still lands **Bardic Inspiration** on Bo for the bit swing.

**[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]]** casts **Wild Shape.** He picks *giant wolf spider* on the basis that he has, in his life, seen giant garbage-adjacent spiders in Boston and he has *just seen this wolf* in front of him. GM lets him.

The transformation is agonizing. Bo describes it as ribs cracking open into eight legs while still wearing clothes. He vomits from the pain the moment he shifts. Two of the spider's legs are just kind of hovering off the ground because he doesn't know how to use them yet.

Distance to wolf: 26 feet. Spider movement: 40 feet, no jump. **Mighty Deed** to leap it. Success. Bite the wolf in the face. Hits for 5 damage; the wolf saves the poison Con save.

Then Bo rolls badly on the concentration-adjacent stability check and **reverts** to his human form mid-bite, still with his teeth in the wolf's neck, still crying from the pain.

> *"He watches Bo turn back into a man."*
> — **GM**

> *"Why you didn't say this by the form, eh?"*
> — **[[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]**

**[[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]** — winding up for a slap shot — sees this happen. Loses the beat.

**[[Player Characters/Lee Smith\|Lee]]** stays in the shadows off to the side. **Winter wolf does not have darkvision** by the stat block the GM is running (a probable RAW oversight — DM houserules to keep it that way for the fight). Lee is invisible to it.

**Round 2**

Wolf is at 3 HP after Moonbeam and knives and everyone's contributions. Lee lines up a **sneak attack** from concealment.

The glint of light through the wolf's eye. Killing blow. Delivered from complete silence.

> *"Everybody is just horrified by whatever the fuck Bo is doing, right? While people are still yelling about why the fuck did you change back — it's going to be the smallest glint of light just punctures his eye and just keels over. And I would like to imagine there's not a lot of fanfare. You guys are still just yelling about Bo."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Lee Smith\|Lee]]**

The wolf collapses onto Bo. Both go down in a heap.

The wolf **evaporates like dry ice.** So does its blood, which wipes the chalk outlines from the ground. Bo, mouth still full of dissipating winter-wolf blood, takes 1 more point of damage as it evaporates in his teeth.

**[[Player Characters/Leonard Holloway\|Leonard]]** photographs the wolf carcass with his Polaroid One Step *just* before it disappears. Photo captured in party journal.

He then screenshots the arcane sigil as well.

## Part Eight: O'Malley Learns the Truth

[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]] rounds the corner. He sees:

- Vomit on the crime scene
- The chalk outlines wiped away
- Frost on Pierre's legs
- Bo, human again, spitting the last of the wolf out of his mouth

> *"I want to know why it is that I asked you to check this crime scene out and not disturb it, and one of you is puking all over it, and someone has been scrubbing the fucking ground and removing the chalk lines."*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]**

Chandler hands him **two Polaroids** — the wolf and the sigil — and starts talking.

O'Malley doesn't buy it. Chandler decides the fastest path to buy-in is a demonstration.

He casts **Prestidigitation** and lights his thumb on fire like a lighter.

Then **Command** on O'Malley: *"Sit."* O'Malley **saves.**

Then **Detect Thoughts.** O'Malley does not save.

### What Chandler Reads

> *"You know what fucking gets me? The pager. The fucking pager. Thirty years a cop. Well, 18, but it feels like 30. And the thing that's going to put me in the ground is a goddamn beeper. Kid comes in, says there's a man, Roger. Roger fucking G-Man. What kind of a — that's not a name. That's what you say when someone asks you your name and you panic."*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]** *(inner monologue)*

> *"I had a dog smarter than whoever cooked that up. God rest him. Brennan. Best dog I ever had."*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]** *(inner monologue)*

Chandler probes deeper. O'Malley *feels* this — describes it later as *"someone taking their finger and pushing it into your skull like your bone is made out of butter and twisting it around in there."*

His hand goes to his service weapon. He draws on Chandler while still sitting on the ground.

The deeper thought Chandler surfaces:

> *"Maureen took the kids to her sister's in Quincy. Quincy might as well be the moon. Declan Junior — eight years old — he doesn't even call. He calls me by my first name now, like we're at the wake, like I'm already in the box, like he's just being polite to a corpse. And Swerzy — she says nothing. Which is worse. She just looks at me with her mother's eyes, that same look, the one that says I had you measured years ago and you came up short."*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]** *(inner monologue)*

At this point [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] drops his glamour to full Orc form and casts **Halo of Spores** so O'Malley can see the party is not entirely human. O'Malley pivots his service weapon toward Bo.

Chandler ends concentration on Detect Thoughts.

> *"Get out of my goddamn head."*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]**

> *"Do you know what that fucking feels like? Like someone taking their finger and pushing it into your skull like your bone is made out of butter and twisting it around in there?"*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]**

Chandler talks him down. Persuasion. O'Malley puts the gun away, stands up, wipes off his knees, and takes a long swig from his flask.

Chandler hands him a photo of the wolf. And three Flintstones vitamins.

> *"They are useful against the forces we're fighting."*
> — **[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]**

O'Malley groans. Grinds the three vitamins Kelsey had given her father into the wind. Bo hands him three more. Bo also squeezes calamine lotion into his hand for good measure.

> *"You know, I started this night off thinking that a teenage girl saying a fella gave her drugs to keep the devil off was the worst thing that would fucking happen to me."*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]**

O'Malley's plan:
