# Kelsey Carey

![Kelseycarey.webp](/img/user/Images/NPCs/Kelseycarey.webp)

> [!info] NPC Summary
> **Role:** Witness to the Charlestown Cold Incident; the only person to *see* the figure
> **Affiliation:** Carey family, Charlestown
> **Location:** 14 Quarter Street, Charlestown
> **First Appeared:** Referenced in [[Sessions/Session 03 - Squiggly Bigglies\|Session 03 - Squiggly Bigglies]] (via [[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]'s file)

## Description

**Appearance:** Not yet described on-screen. Daughter of [[NPCs/Charlestown/Robert Carey\|Robert Carey]] and [[NPCs/Charlestown/Sandra Carey\|Sandra Carey]]. Old enough to articulate clearly; young enough that [[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]] called her a "sharp kid."

**Personality:** Observant. Articulate. Waited for her father to leave the room before she gave her statement — she didn't want him to hear what she'd seen. **Did not fully go under** like the rest of the family did. Kept coming back to consciousness.

**Voice / Mannerisms:** Direct. Lets the silence around the strange parts do its own work.

## What She Told O'Malley

> *"It didn't go all the way under like they did. It kept coming back. I saw it. It was tall, too tall for the room, standing by the TV very still. It felt like it was writing something down — like we, as in the family, weren't the point. What we were feeling was the point."*
> — **Kelsey Carey**

> *"Sharp kid. I believe her."*
> — **[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]** *(field note, attached)*

---

## Why She Matters

Kelsey is the only witness across all three Cold Incidents who:

1. **Did not lose consciousness completely** during the encounter
2. **Saw the figure** physically present in the room
3. **Articulated its purpose** — *the family was not the point, what they were feeling was the point*

That last detail confirms what the party started to suspect at the Soldier's Monument forensics scene: the entity is doing **observation and data collection on emotional response**, not predation per se. The Carey family's grief over the World Series was the *signal,* and Kelsey was the only sensor that didn't shut off.

> [!note] Now interviewed in [[Sessions/Session 04 - Roger G-Man\|Session 04 - Roger G-Man]]
> Kelsey is a **17-year-old debate-club and school-election extracurricular kid**, plays D&D, has *drawn multiple sketches of the entity* from memory. Firm handshake. Waits for her father to leave the room before she gives her real account.

## [[Sessions/Session 04 - Roger G-Man\|Session 04 - Roger G-Man]]

Kelsey rounded the corner mid-interview and shook [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]'s hand. Cracked jokes about her father's ego. Once he'd left for the kitchen, she gave Chandler the useful account of the incident:

> *"We all saw it. I know we did, because I heard them talking about it, but they didn't see it like I saw it. I didn't go all the way under. I kept coming back to. And I saw it. Standing by the TV. It was too tall. Made out of cold. Felt like it was writing something down."*
> — **Kelsey Carey**

Then the key detail — *why* the entity's Enchantment failed to fully take her:

> *"I felt like it was draining, like my emotions. I definitely felt things. I just didn't feel the things that I wanted to feel."*
> — **Kelsey Carey**

> *"What did you feel when Buckner missed that ball?"*
> — **[[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]]**

> *"Oh, I don't give a shit. That's baseball. That's dad stuff."*
> — **Kelsey Carey**

She was emotionally neutral toward the game her parents were mourning. The entity was after *their* grief, not hers. That's what let her keep coming to.

She pulled a notebook out of her school bag and gave [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] one of her sketches of the entity — described by Chandler as *"Jack Frost and Edward from Twilight had a baby and it knew how to act."* This is the party's first visual reference for [[NPCs/Winter Court/The Recorder\|The Recorder]].

### Later That Night

[[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] returned to the door disguised as *"Roger G-Man,"* claiming to be doing a youth recycling outreach on behalf of the City of Boston. Kelsey answered. She was not fooled — she immediately pointed out that Boston is *made of landfill,* asked for a pamphlet, and thought Roger G-Man was, in her words, *"only the second weirdest person that I've met today, so you're good."*

She accepted **three Flintstones Chewable Vitamins** from Bo *"to keep the devil away,"* took down his callback (which was actually [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]'s pager number, later discarded), and closed the door.

The vitamins made their way to her father, who confronted [[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]] with them later that night, which triggered O'Malley's arrival at the Site 1 crime scene, which triggered everything else.

Kelsey is **not** awakened — she was emotionally neutral, not magically resistant. She may still remember Roger G-Man forever.
