# Dr. Richard Sparks

![Richard Sparks.webp|Dr. Richard Sparks](/img/user/Images/NPCs/Richard%20Sparks.webp)

Harvard professor of history with an office on the second floor of Robinson Hall. Swept silver hair, gray beard, brown spectacles, tweed jacket, rust waistcoat, and dark green bow tie. First met in [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]].

## What the Party Knows

Sparks interrupted [[NPCs/Summer Court/Lady Celadine Ashe\|Lady Celadine Ashe]]'s hearing at Bo's apartment, challenged her proposed bargain, and obtained an adjournment. He describes himself as one of Boston's guardians and says Boston is a living magical city that gathers people with potential. He has companions named Leonard and Edwin; their full identities remain unconfirmed.

His magical aura overwhelmed Chandler's Detect Magic. He recognizes Queen Mab's foremost scout from Kelsey's drawing and the [[Campaign Notes/Winter Scribing Shard\|Winter Scribing Shard]], and says Winter seeks and feeds on sorrow. He will research the Green One before offering an identification. He encourages investigating [[Locations/Boston Garden\|Boston Garden]] on the party's existing evidence.

His assistance depends on availability. No standing legal contract was agreed. Bo has his Harvard card; Sparks has Jacques's pager as a callback contact. His answering machine explicitly denies that messages constitute consent to Fey obligations. The transcript does not preserve a complete office phone number.
