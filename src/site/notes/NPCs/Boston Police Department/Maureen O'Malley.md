# Maureen O'Malley

[[NPCs/Boston Police Department/Detective O'Malley\|Detective O'Malley]]'s wife. Previously known to Chandler through the detective's thoughts; encountered directly in [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]].

On November 15, Chandler finds her and the children outside O'Malley's home in a wood-paneled station wagon. They came to check on him because he is not answering the phone or door. His usual bars either haven't seen him or won't say whether they have.

She exchanges contact information with Chandler. They agree to tell each other if O'Malley calls or returns. His whereabouts remain unknown. No appearance, precise telephone number, or new details about the children are established in the scene.
