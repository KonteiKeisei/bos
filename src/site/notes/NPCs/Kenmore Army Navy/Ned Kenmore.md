# Ned Kenmore

Proprietor of [[Locations/Kenmore Army Navy\|Kenmore Army Navy]], encountered in [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]]. A friendly Marine veteran who trades service stories with Chandler, helps with the search for iron weapons, and brings out Northern Fleet Service Bayonets. He sells the party equipment but has no arrows suitable for Jacques's longbow. No supernatural affiliation is established.
