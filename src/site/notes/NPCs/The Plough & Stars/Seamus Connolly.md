# Seamus Connolly

![SeamusConnely.webp|Seamus Connolly|400](/img/user/Images/NPCs/SeamusConnely.webp)

Owner and bartender at [[Locations/The Plough & Stars\|The Plough & Stars]], 912 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge. Interviewed by Chandler in [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]]. He cooks Bo's steak on a portable burner behind the bar, complains about the orders, and makes them anyway. Recognizes paying customers and is protective of his reputation.

## What He Tells the Party

Seamus says he left [[NPCs/Cambridge/Dennis Fahey\|Dennis Fahey]], a regular he grew up with, watching the bar while he smoked. He returned to four men frozen at their seats, with the taps and their drinks frozen despite the heating. He remembers calling the police around midnight. Chandler has a report placing the discovery and response the following morning.

After Chandler presses him, Seamus admits that his memory is unreliable. He remembers remembering the night clearly when he spoke to the police. He later wiped frost markings from the mirror, felt something happen, and has found that night's memories getting worse ever since. The rest of his memory works normally. He asks to be kept out of Chandler's reporting.

Fahey has not been back. Seamus remembers an old Ellery Street address, possibly 150, from about thirty years ago. He does not claim it is current.

Bo offers four Flintstones vitamins under the name Bob, spelled B-O-H-B. Seamus has heard about someone matching Bo's description giving vitamins to children. He refuses them and shows the revolver under the bar. Chandler leaves $20 for a $10 drinks tab.
