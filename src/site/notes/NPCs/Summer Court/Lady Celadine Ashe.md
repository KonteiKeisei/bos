# Lady Celadine Ashe

Summer Court representative first encountered at Bo's apartment in [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]]. A red-haired woman in a burgundy suit with green and antique-gold embroidery, carrying a leather folio and attended by miniature court clerks with typewriters. Name spelling follows the clearest introduction in the transcript.

She charges Bo with injuriously dismissing Summer's observer. Her first proposed settlement requires a replacement radio, three sunsets of renewed hospitality with up to eight attendants, and acknowledgement of the dismissal. Her alternate offer exchanges scientific recognition of Bo's mycelium for a broad prohibition on interfering with Summer-authorized work.

She invokes the Fey courtesy of answer, treating silence after formal accusation as admission, and claims prior Fey jurisdiction over Boston's land. [[NPCs/Harvard/Dr. Richard Sparks\|Dr. Richard Sparks]] exposes undefined obligations in her proposal. She adjourns without securing agreement and can return. The clerks leave their equipment behind.

The party suspects Summer's interest overlaps the Winter investigation. Her exact role in the cold incidents is unproven.
