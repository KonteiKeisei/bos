# Frank Galloway

Night-shift security contact at [[Locations/Boston Garden\|Boston Garden]], previously introduced to [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]]. [[NPCs/Boston Bruins/Harry Sinden\|Harry Sinden]] tells Jacques to approach Frank about after-hours ice access in [[Sessions/Session 05 - Boston Jesus\|Session 05 - Boston Jesus]]. The party has not yet spoken with him on-screen or obtained that permission.

## Session 06: Frank Gallo

Called Frank Gallo throughout [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]]. He is 58, has worked Garden security for 22 years, and is sturdy with a slight stoop, thinning salt-and-pepper hair, and heavy brows. He knows Jacques well and supports what he did in the incident that led to his suspension.

Jacques successfully persuades him to allow four friends to stay with him after the November 15 game, saying he wants to skate with them and remember playing hockey. Frank is wary of strangers interested in locked doors or contraband, especially a reporter. The arrangement is to report to security as the crowd leaves, wait in a back room for an hour and a half, then meet Frank by the rink shield. Access is agreed; the game and investigation are still ahead.
