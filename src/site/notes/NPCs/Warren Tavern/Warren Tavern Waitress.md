# Warren Tavern Waitress

![Session 06 - Warren Tavern Waitress.png|The waitress at Warren Tavern|400](/img/user/Images/Sessions/Session%2006%20-%20Warren%20Tavern%20Waitress.png)

Serves the group at [[Locations/Warren Tavern\|Warren Tavern]] in [[Sessions/Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee\|Session 06 - Who the Fuck Is Lee]]. Auburn hair pinned with a plastic comb, a white blouse, black trousers, and a burgundy cardigan with its sleeves pushed up. Gold hoop earrings and a wedding band. Her apron holds an order pad, three pens, and a cigarette packet.

She checks the table and replaces Leonard's dirty fork before taking the orders. Gives them a booth, recommends the chowder and haddock, and brings salt and vinegar for the fries. She speaks with a Boston accent and calls customers hon or sweetie.

She sorts out the potato skins as a shared appetizer and offers Pierre mashed turnip as his haddock side. Jacques asks for pate chinois and ends up ordering shepherd's pie. She asks whether he's a hockey player; he says he tries.
