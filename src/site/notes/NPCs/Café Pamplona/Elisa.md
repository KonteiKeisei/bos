# Elisa

![Eliza.webp](/img/user/Images/NPCs/Eliza.webp)

> [!info] NPC Summary
> **Role:** Barista at [[Locations/Café Pamplona\|Café Pamplona]]
> **Affiliation:** Café Pamplona
> **Location:** 12 Bow Street, Cambridge
> **Ethnicity:** Italian (self-described "full Italian")
> **First Appeared:** [[Sessions/Session 04 - Roger G-Man\|Session 04 - Roger G-Man]]

## Description

**Appearance:** Dark-haired barista, warm-and-cutting register at the same time.

**Personality:** Insulting-hospitable. She takes coffee orders with an editorial. She asked [[Player Characters/Bohdan Thorruk\|Bo]] about his ethnicity mid-order the way another person might ask if he wanted cream, and closed the loop with *"you're Canadian? Well, calm the hell down."* She yanks a cookie out of a customer's reach at unnatural speed if she thinks another customer is trying to steal a piece. She calls the customer she doesn't approve of *sweetie.*

**Voice / Mannerisms:** Direct. Loud enough to be heard across the café. Sarcasm delivered with sincere warmth.

---

## Session 04

Working the counter alone on the night [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] brought the party in for the [[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]] interview. Handled orders in this order:

- Two black Colombians (Chandler + Bo)
- A tall cold glass of milk with an oatmeal-raisin cookie the size of Leonard's face
- Water for Lee
- Black coffee with two sugars for [[Player Characters/Pierre-Jackson Laferrière\|Pierre]] *("Prince — you want another coffee, Purple Rain?")*
- A latte with a chocolate croissant for [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] when he arrived late
- A beignet for Pierre later

$5.75 for the first round.

She observed [[Player Characters/Chandler T. Harrow\|Chandler]] attempting to sleight-of-hand a piece of Leonard's cookie and yanked the cookie away *"faster than the speed of light — like someone doing the diabetes marathon race for life."*

She also, correctly, identified [[Player Characters/Jacques le Coq\|Jacques]] on sight as *"professional hockey player Jacques the Rooster the Cock,"* and commented, food-in-mouth, about how *"there's something about an old-fashioned romp of fisticuffs that just gets the juices flowing, if you know what I mean."*

She served [[NPCs/Harvard/Patricia Osei\|Patricia Osei]] her usual small lobster-tail pastry and a fancy coffee with too much cream — implying Osei is a regular at the café.
